# MedMinds Facebook creative house style

Audited against the public MedMinds Learning Centre Facebook Page on 13 September 2026.

## Default format
- 1080 x 1080 square for feed creatives.
- Keep generous edge safe zones; do not crowd the canvas.

## Brand hierarchy
- Official MedMinds Learning Centre logo: top-left or top-centre, approximately 10–15% of canvas width.
- Headline: bold, all-caps sans-serif, navy, approximately 66–88 px on a 1080 px square depending on length.
- Supporting benefit text: approximately 30 px; maximum three concise points.
- CTA: green rounded pill, approximately 25 px.
- Category/eyebrow: teal, all caps, approximately 20 px with generous letter spacing.

## Palette
- Navy: #17324d
- Deep navy: #10283f
- Teal: #178e8b
- Light teal: #dff4f2
- MedMinds green: #0d725f
- Cream/off-white: #f5f7f4
- Light grey: #edf1f2
- White cards: #ffffff

## Structure
- White or cream cards over light grey/teal backgrounds.
- Rounded corners, subtle shadows and clean spacing.
- Feature/benefit creatives use up to three green-check benefit lines.
- FAQ/hook creatives prioritise one large readable headline over extra copy.
- Photo creatives keep the subject on the right and use a clean white information card on the left.
- Avoid stacks of books, anatomy-poster clutter, busy desks, excessive decorative elements, tiny copy and long paragraphs on the image.

The post caption carries the detail. The image should communicate one idea at a glance.
